# Alert

Displays a callout for user attention. Compose an `Alert` from an optional icon,
an `AlertTitle`, and an `AlertDescription`.

<Example>
  <Box width="400px">
    <Alert>
      <Lucide.Terminal />
      <AlertTitle>Heads up!</AlertTitle>
      <AlertDescription>
        You can add components to your app using the CLI.
      </AlertDescription>
    </Alert>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Alert>
    <Lucide.Terminal />
    <AlertTitle>Heads up!</AlertTitle>
    <AlertDescription>You can add components to your app using the CLI.</AlertDescription>
  </Alert>
  ```
</Example>

## Destructive

Use the `destructive` variant for errors and failed operations.

<Example>
  <Box width="400px">
    <Alert variant="destructive">
      <Lucide.CircleAlert />
      <AlertTitle>Unable to process your payment.</AlertTitle>
      <AlertDescription>
        Please verify your billing information and try again.
      </AlertDescription>
    </Alert>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Alert variant="destructive">
    <Lucide.CircleAlert />
    <AlertTitle>Unable to process your payment.</AlertTitle>
    <AlertDescription>Please verify your billing information and try again.</AlertDescription>
  </Alert>
  ```
</Example>

## Without icon

Icons are optional children — omit the icon and the content spans the full width.

<Example>
  <Box width="400px">
    <Alert>
      <AlertTitle>Scheduled maintenance</AlertTitle>
      <AlertDescription>
        The service will be unavailable on Sunday from 2–4 AM UTC.
      </AlertDescription>
    </Alert>
  </Box>

  ```jsx
  <Alert>
    <AlertTitle>Scheduled maintenance</AlertTitle>
    <AlertDescription>The service will be unavailable on Sunday from 2–4 AM UTC.</AlertDescription>
  </Alert>
  ```
</Example>

`Alert`, `AlertTitle`, and `AlertDescription` all accept standard `div` props
including `className`, and expose `data-slot="alert"`, `data-slot="alert-title"`,
and `data-slot="alert-description"` attributes for styling.
